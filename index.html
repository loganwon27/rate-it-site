Rate It is an app where people post photos and everyone else rates them from 1 to 10. Use it on iPhone or right here on the web.

Contact us

Email rateit.supportemail@gmail.com. We reply within 2 days, and review reports of abuse within 24 hours.

How rating works

  • Swipe up through Home and tap or slide across 1–10 to rate a post.
  • Once you rate, you see the average, where the post ranks and how everyone voted.
  • You can rate each post once, and ratings are locked once given.
  • Anonymous ratings count toward totals, but the poster never sees who gave them.

Report abuse or block someone

  • Tap the ••• menu on any post, comment or profile and choose Report. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Posts reported by several people are hidden automatically until they're reviewed.
  • Choose Block from the same menu. You won't see each other's posts, and they can't follow you. Manage blocks in Settings → Blocked users.
  • For anything urgent, email rateit.supportemail@gmail.com.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, ratings, likes, comments and follows. Can't get into the app? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Common questions

Can I change a rating? No. Ratings are locked so results stay honest.

Who can see my posts? Everyone except people you've blocked. Posts where you hid your username don't show your name or appear on your profile for others.

I forgot my password. On the log-in screen tap Forgot password?.
