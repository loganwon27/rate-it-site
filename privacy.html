Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is stored securely by our authentication provider and is never visible to anyone, including us.
  • Profile: your username, and the photo, bio and interests you choose to add.
  • Content: photos you post (with their category and caption), ratings, likes, comments, follows, blocks and reports.
  • Settings: your privacy and notification preferences and when you agreed to the terms.

We don't collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How it's used

Only to run Rate It: showing posts and results, building your feed from your interests and the people you follow, sending in-app notifications, preventing duplicate ratings, and keeping the community safe (reviewing reports, enforcing the terms). We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Other users see your username, profile photo, bio, public posts, comments and follower counts.
  • Your email is never shown to other users.
  • Anonymous ratings count toward totals but are never shown with your name. We keep the link to your account internally only to stop duplicate ratings and abuse.
  • Posts you mark as anonymous don't show your username and don't appear on your profile for others.
  • People you block can't see your profile or posts.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) on servers in the United States. Photos are stored in a way that anyone with a photo's exact link could open it, so don't post anything you need to keep private.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, ratings, likes, comments and follows.

Children

Rate It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.
